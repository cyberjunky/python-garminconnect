   - `public.coach_messages`: ID, `conversation_id`, `owner_id`, `sequence >= 1`, `role in ('user', 'assistant', 'system')`, `check (char_length(content) <= 4000)`, clave foránea compuesta `(conversation_id, owner_id) references public.coach_conversations(id, owner_id) on delete cascade`, unique `(conversation_id, sequence)`.
   - `public.coach_operations`: Clave primaria `(owner_id, operation_id)`, `status in ('in_progress', 'completed', 'failed')`, `lease_until`, clave foránea compuesta `(conversation_id, owner_id) references public.coach_conversations(id, owner_id) on delete cascade`.
   - `public.coach_imports`: ID, `owner_id`, `source_id`, `source_digest`, `status in ('imported', 'skipped', 'failed')`, unique `(owner_id, source_id, source_digest)`.
   - `public.coach_profile_links`: `auth_user_id` PK references `auth.users(id)` en cascada, `profile_id uuid not null`.
